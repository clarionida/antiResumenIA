Remove AI from Google and see just links again

Search Without AI is a free Chrome extension that hides Google's AI Overview, AI Mode, ads and other boxes. You choose what to hide.

What you can hide

Each option is a checkbox you can turn on or off. Changes apply instantly, no reload needed.

Artificial intelligence

  • AI Overviews on
  • The AI Mode tab and button on

Ads and shopping

  • Ads and sponsored results on
  • Product grids and carousels

Other Google sections

  • “People also ask”
  • Videos and short videos
  • Images between results
  • Maps and nearby places
  • “Discussions and forums”
  • “People also search for”

And also

  • Your list of blocked sites (for example, pinterest.com)
  • “Web only” mode
  • “Only pre-AI content” date filter
  • A switch to pause everything

That easy

  1. Install it in Chrome. It's ready to go: it hides AI Overviews, AI Mode and ads.
  2. Search on Google as usual. At the bottom left you'll see how many items were hidden.
  3. Adjust anything from the toolbar icon or in “More options”.

Your privacy comes first

  • Your options are saved with Chrome sync. The counter stays in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove the AI Overview from Google?

Install the free Search Without AI extension in Chrome. From then on, the “AI Overview” box no longer appears in your Google searches, and the rest of the results stay the same.

Can I turn off Google's AI without installing anything?

Google has no switch to turn it off. You can pick the “Web” tab on every search or set up a custom search engine with the udm=14 parameter. Search Without AI does it automatically and lets you choose what to hide.

Is Search Without AI free?

Yes, 100% free: no premium version, no locked features and no ads. Donations are optional.

Does it collect my data or my searches?

No. It doesn't collect, send or sell any data, makes no network requests and has no analytics. It only reads the Google results page in your browser to hide what you chose.

Does it work with my country's Google?

Yes. It works on more than 40 Google domains, such as google.com, google.co.uk, google.ca, google.com.au, google.es and google.com.mx, with Google in English, Spanish or Portuguese.

Can I see what was hidden?

Yes. A small notice on the Google page says “N items hidden · Show”. Click “Show” to see everything that was hidden, outlined with a dashed border.

Does it work in Edge, Brave or Opera?

Those browsers are based on Chrome and accept its extensions, so it should work, although it's tested in Chrome. It only acts when you search on Google.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Chrome for Android and iPhone doesn't support extensions. It works in desktop Chrome.

What happens if Google changes its page?

Google changes its page often. The extension is reviewed every month and updated when needed. If something stops being hidden, you can report it with the “Report a problem” link in the options.

Install

Chrome Web Store: coming soon. The extension is under review.

Meanwhile, you can install it manually:

  1. Download the project: download .zip.
  2. Unzip it and keep the folder somewhere permanent, for example in Documents.
  3. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder that contains manifest.json.
  5. Pin the icon from the puzzle-piece menu.

Don't delete or move the folder afterwards: Chrome uses it from there.
