Frequently asked questions

Artificial intelligence

How do I remove the AI Overview from Google?

Install the free Search Without AI extension in Chrome. From then on, the “AI Overview” box no longer appears in your Google searches, and the rest of the results stay the same.

How do I remove Google’s “AI Mode”?

The Search Without AI extension hides the “AI Mode” tab and its button next to the search box. It’s on by default.

Can I turn off Google’s AI without installing anything?

Google has no switch to turn it off. You can pick the “Web” tab on every search or set up a custom search engine with the udm=14 parameter. The Search Without AI extension does it automatically and lets you choose what to hide.

Ads and shopping

How do I remove ads from Google search results?

The Search Without AI extension hides ads and sponsored results (“Sponsored”) at the top and bottom of the results page. It’s on by default, so you only see regular results.

Is it an ad blocker like AdBlock or uBlock?

Not exactly. The Search Without AI extension only cleans up the Google results page: it doesn’t block ads on other websites or YouTube, and it doesn’t block connections, it just hides what you don’t want to see. If you already use an ad blocker, you can use both at the same time.

Can I hide products, prices and stores from the results?

Yes. In the Search Without AI extension, turn on “Hide shopping blocks” and product grids and carousels with prices disappear, even when they have no heading. Google’s “Shopping” tab still works normally if you want to buy something.

Other Google sections

How do I remove “People also ask”?

In the Search Without AI extension’s options, turn on “Hide “People also ask””. The list of expandable questions disappears and the results move up.

Can I hide Google’s videos, images and maps?

Yes. The Search Without AI extension lets you hide each one separately: video and short-video carousels, image boxes between results, and the places block with the map and nearby businesses. The “Videos” and “Images” tabs and Google Maps keep working.

How do I remove “People also search for” or related searches?

In the Search Without AI extension, turn on “Hide “People also search for””. The suggestions at the bottom of the page disappear, and so does the box that shows up under a result when you go back. Pagination stays.

How do I remove “Discussions and forums” or Reddit results?

The Search Without AI extension’s “Hide “Discussions and forums”” option removes the block of threads from Reddit, Quora and other forums. If you also don’t want regular Reddit results, add reddit.com to your blocked sites.

Blocked sites

Can I block sites like Pinterest from Google results?

Yes. In the Search Without AI extension, open “My blocked sites” and type the domain, for example pinterest.com. Its results disappear from your searches, subdomains included. You can add up to 300 sites and remove them anytime.

Advanced options

What is “Web only” mode (udm=14)?

It’s an advanced option of the Search Without AI extension that opens your searches straight in Google’s “Web” view, which shows just results with links, by adding udm=14 to the search address. It also removes other panels, like info cards or weather. Images, News, Videos, Maps and Shopping stay the same. It’s off by default.

Can I see only pre-AI pages, for example from before 2023?

Yes. The Search Without AI extension’s “Only pre-AI content” option adds Google’s date filter to your searches to show pages published before the day you choose (January 1, 2023 by default). Google estimates each page’s date, so it may not be exact. It’s off by default.

Usage, privacy and compatibility

Is the Search Without AI extension free?

Yes, the Search Without AI extension is 100% free: no premium version, no locked features and no ads. Donations are optional.

Does it collect my data or my searches?

No. The Search Without AI extension doesn’t collect, send or sell any data, makes no network requests and has no analytics. It only reads the Google results page in your browser to hide what you chose.

Can I see what was hidden or pause the extension?

Yes. With the Search Without AI extension, a small notice on the Google page says “N items hidden · Show” and lets you see what was hidden, outlined with a dashed border. The switch in the toolbar icon pauses everything instantly without losing your settings.

Are my settings saved across my computers?

If you use Chrome sync, the Search Without AI extension’s options and blocked sites are saved to your account and show up on your other computers. You can also export them to a file and import them in another browser.

Does it work with my country’s Google?

Yes. The Search Without AI extension works on more than 40 Google domains, such as google.com, google.co.uk, google.ca, google.com.au, google.es and google.com.mx, with Google in English, Spanish or Portuguese.

Does it work in Edge, Brave or Opera?

Those browsers are based on Chrome and accept its extensions, so the Search Without AI extension should work, although it’s tested in Chrome. It only acts when you search on Google.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Chrome for Android and iPhone doesn’t support extensions, so the Search Without AI extension works only in desktop Chrome.

What happens if Google changes its page?

Google changes its page often. The Search Without AI extension is reviewed every month and updated when needed. If something stops being hidden, you can report it with the “Report a problem” link in the options.

How is Search Without AI different from other extensions?

The Search Without AI extension is Spanish-first, it doesn’t just remove AI (ads, shopping and other sections too), it lets you choose what to hide one by one, it includes a blocked-sites list, it tracks nothing and its code is public on GitHub.

Install

Chrome Web Store: coming soon. The extension is under review.

Meanwhile, you can install it manually:

  1. Download the project: download .zip.
  2. Unzip it and keep the folder somewhere permanent, for example in Documents.
  3. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder that contains manifest.json.
  5. Pin the icon from the puzzle-piece menu.

Don't delete or move the folder afterwards: Chrome uses it from there.
